Index

The index page's main goal is to grab new prospective viewers' attention.

In line with this, the top of the page includes a picture of Samuel Fuller alongside a quote from his autobiography about his film and his subsequent interrogation by the Pentagon: the aspect of The Steel Helmet's production most likely to grasp a reader's attention. This is followed by a description of what the reader can expect to find on the site, followed by a filterable table of films they may find interesting.

Findings

The findings are ultimately split into two templates. The first template is the main page, displaying all 32 films alongside one another -- each one clickable. The second template is uniform across all 32 films, allowing the user to look more deeply into any one film they wish via clicking on whichever one looks interesting.
